Privacy Policy for GlowSnake.io & Atesh Studio Applications

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 | Developer: Atesh Studio (Founder: Atəş Balıyev) | Official Website: atesh.site

Atesh Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile games and applications including GlowSnake.io, Bondify, E5cape, and TələbəX. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect

GlowSnake.io does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts.

  • Gameplay & Local Data: Your high scores, customized snake skins, unlocked achievements, coins, and level progress are stored solely on your local device (via local app storage). We do not send your game saves to private external servers.
  • Crash Logs & Performance: Basic non-identifiable technical data (e.g., device model, operating system version) may be processed automatically by Google Play to ensure game stability.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

GlowSnake.io integrates Google AdMob to serve rewarded video ads and monetize gameplay fairly:

  • Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and general diagnostic info to provide relevant ads and detect click fraud or bot activity.
  • For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google Advertising Policies.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

Our game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. The game contains family-friendly arcade content with cartoon graphics and does not contain violence, profanity, or adult themes.

4. Device Permissions

GlowSnake.io requests only the minimal permissions required to function properly:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load Google AdMob rewarded videos and verify network availability.
  • VIBRATE: Optional tactile feedback during snake boost and in-game collisions.
  • AD_ID: Standard Android 14 permission for Google Play Ads SDK.

5. Data Security & Data Deletion

Because no personal data is collected on external servers by Atesh Studio, your personal information is completely secure. Local game preferences remain on your device and can be reset at any time by clearing the application storage. For complete data deletion policies, visit our developer portal at https://atesh.site/data-deletion.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted.
