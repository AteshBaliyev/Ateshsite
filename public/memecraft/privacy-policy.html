Welcome to Meme Craft (Meme Craft: Social & Studio), published by Baliyews Studio ("we", "our", or "us"). We are firmly committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines clearly, transparently, and comprehensively how Meme Craft handles data, how photos are processed locally on your device, our integration with Google AdMob, and your complete rights under applicable global privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Privacy-First Architecture: Meme Craft does NOT require user accounts, does NOT collect your personal contact information, and performs 100% of all image editing and meme generation locally on your hardware. We do not operate external servers that store your photos.

1 No User Accounts & No Personal Server Storage

Meme Craft is designed as a standalone, client-side utility and meme studio. To use any feature of the application:

  • You are never required to create an account, register, or log in.
  • We do not collect, request, or store personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or social media credentials.
  • Baliyews Studio does not operate private backend cloud servers or databases to retain, catalog, or harvest user personal profiles or private photo collections.

2 Photos & Device Gallery Access (Android Photo Picker & MediaStore)

Meme Craft allows users to create memes by either using pre-loaded templates or importing pictures from their own device storage:

  • Privacy-Preserving Photo Picker: When you choose to use an image from your device gallery, Meme Craft utilizes modern, standard Android system components (such as the system Android Photo Picker and scoped MediaStore APIs). The application only gains temporary access to the specific photo file you explicitly select.
  • 100% Local On-Device Processing: All image cropping, caption text rendering, font adjustments, sticker overlays, and canvas composition happen strictly locally on your device using your device's local memory and graphics processing unit.
  • Saving to Device: When you tap the "Save" or "Share" button, the resulting generated meme is saved locally to your device storage (under Pictures/MemeCraft or your device's standard public Pictures directory via the Android MediaStore API).
  • Zero Remote Uploads: Your private gallery photos, camera captures, and custom memes are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to external servers or third parties. Your media remains entirely under your personal control on your hardware.

3 Local Storage & On-Device Preferences Only

To ensure a responsive and smooth user experience, Meme Craft stores minimal, non-personally identifiable operational states exclusively in local device sandbox storage (such as SharedPreferences and local application cache):

  • Saved Favorites: Meme templates or creations that you explicitly mark as favorite for quick access.
  • Application Language & Theme: Your selected interface language and UI preferences.
  • 64-bit Viewed Meme Hash List: To deliver an engaging, non-repetitive discovery feed, the app maintains an anonymized list of 64-bit numerical hashes corresponding to viewed meme URLs. This list prevents the feed from presenting duplicate memes and contains no personal identity.

All of the above data stays 100% on your local device. You can reset or wipe this data at any time from within the application by navigating to Settings > Clear Cache / Reset Feed.

4 Third-Party Content APIs (Public SFW Meme Feeds)

To provide a rich library of trending meme templates and discovery images, Meme Craft fetches publicly available, Safe-For-Work (SFW) content dynamically over secure encrypted connections (HTTPS) from reputable public meme APIs:

  • Content Sources: Public APIs including Imgflip API, Memegen API, and public Reddit SFW meme endpoints.
  • Data Transmission: When these feeds are queried, only standard HTTP request headers necessary to fetch public image assets are communicated. We never attach user identity, device serial numbers, or location parameters to content queries.

5 Advertising (Google AdMob SDK)

To provide Meme Craft as a free application, we integrate the Google AdMob SDK, an industry-standard mobile advertising platform provided by Google LLC. AdMob displays Banner Ads, Rewarded Video Ads, and Native Advanced Ads within the application.

In accordance with Google's policies and global data protection standards (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act - CCPA), Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain pseudonymous device telemetry:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), hardware model, operating system version, and mobile network carrier.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Metrics: Ad loading speed, crash reports, and system performance metrics.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Impressions, click events, and ad view durations, which are used to measure advertising effectiveness, compensate publishers, and prevent fraudulent click activity or bot traffic.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geolocation derived from IP address (not precise GPS) to display regionally relevant advertisements.

Google operates as an independent data controller with respect to advertising telemetry. For complete information on how Google processes, stores, and protects advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Advertising & Privacy Controls: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

7 User Rights, Data Management & Opt-Out Instructions

Users possess complete control over their device data and advertising preferences:

  • Resetting or Deleting Your Android Advertising ID (AAID): You can reset or permanently delete your unique Advertising Identifier at any time directly through your device settings:
    1. Open your Android device Settings.
    2. Navigate to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads depending on your Android version).
    3. Tap "Reset advertising ID" to cycle your identifier, or "Delete advertising ID" to prevent personalized advertising across all apps.
  • Consent Management (EEA / UK / California): Where legally required, Meme Craft displays Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog upon initial launch, allowing users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California to accept, reject, or customize their ad preferences and consent choices.
  • Clearing Application Storage: You can wipe all local favorites, cached meme templates, and viewed URL hashes by:
    • Using the in-app option: Settings > Clear Cache / Reset Feed.
    • Or through system settings: Android Settings > Apps > Meme Craft > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Cache.
  • Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling Meme Craft automatically purges all sandboxed application data and preferences from your device.

8 Data Security Standards

Because Baliyews Studio does not collect or transmit personal information or gallery photographs to cloud servers, your personal data is not exposed to remote server breaches. All network communications for fetching public meme templates and loading AdMob assets are strictly conducted over encrypted Transport Layer Security protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3).
